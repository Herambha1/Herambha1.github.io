Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Amazon Deals Publisher

Amazon Deals Publisher is a personal automation application used to prepare and publish product-deal content to authorized social media accounts.

Information We Access

When you authorize the application to connect to a social media platform, the application may access information and permissions necessary to perform the requested publishing functions.

For Pinterest, this may include information required to create and manage Pins and boards on the authorized Pinterest account.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through connected services is used only to:

  • Publish deal-related content to authorized accounts.
  • Create and manage Pins or other content requested by the account owner.
  • Maintain the application's publishing functionality.

The application does not sell personal information or use connected-account information for unrelated advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials, access tokens, and other sensitive application credentials are stored securely and are not intentionally made publicly available.

The application does not intentionally publish or expose private account credentials.

Third-Party Services

The application may interact with third-party services, including Pinterest and Amazon, as necessary to provide its deal-publishing functionality. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality changes. The latest version will be available at this URL.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the application.
